=======================================================
               DREGITAL STORE LOGO FOLDER
=======================================================

Paste your store logo here with the filename:
logo.png

Requirements:
- Filename must be: logo.png (lowercase or uppercase)
- Format: PNG (transparent background recommended) or JPG/SVG
- Suggested size: Height between 60px and 120px (e.g. 300x80px or 400x100px)

Once you paste logo.png into this folder:
It will automatically display in the header in place of the text "DREGITAL"!
If you remove logo.png, it will automatically switch back to the "DREGITAL" text.
